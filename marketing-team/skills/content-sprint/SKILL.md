# Content Sprint

Usage: `/content-sprint <company>` plus any material: report numbers, call
notes, threads, a finding from the team.

1. **Collect the week's real material first.** Ask Josh, or pull from what
   he gave you: one or two new numbers (with source), something that broke or
   surprised the team, a buyer quote, a thread that's moving. No material
   means no sprint. Say what's missing.
2. **Brief the specialists in parallel**, one real detail per piece:
   - `reddit-operator`: 3 to 5 posts or replies. Most mention nothing.
   - `linkedin-ghostwriter`: 3 posts (mix Josh and the company page) and 3
     comments on other people's posts from the engagement list.
   - `search-writer`: one article, or one brief if the material is thin.
   - `outbound-writer`: two reusable lines for this week's sequences built on
     the same material.
3. **Gate** every piece through `voice-qa`. REWRITE goes back with notes.
4. **Deliver** to `marketing/<company>/week-<yyyy-mm-dd>/` with a
   `schedule.md` (day, channel, account, file) and a `decisions.md`.
5. **Reply** with the schedule and the decisions. Nothing else.
