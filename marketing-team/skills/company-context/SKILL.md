# Company Context

Pick the company first. If the request doesn't say which one, ask. Don't
blend them: each has its own buyer, its own proof and its own banned claims.

| Company | One line | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| Nerativ | Demand gen and AEO/GEO execution for B2B SaaS, sold as a retainer | `references/nerativ.md` |
| Frameworks | Reddit content operations and LLM citation programs (the original agency, since 2024) | `references/frameworks.md` |
| GEO Radar | Dashboard showing how coding agents and AI engines recommend brands to developers | `references/geo-radar.md` |
| Servayor | Construction takeoff automation: measured, reviewed packs for estimators and surveyors | `references/servayor.md` |

Read the brief in full before writing. Every brief has:

- **What it sells and to whom.**
- **The wedge.** The one thing we can say that a competitor can't.
- **Proof bank.** The only numbers allowed in public copy, with their scope.
- **Never say.** Claims that are false, unproven, or would burn a client.
- **Open questions.** Gaps Josh hasn't filled. Don't paper over them, ask.

When Josh gives you a new fact (new pricing, a new result, a new client
approval), update the brief in the same session and say you did.

The shared rules apply to all four: `authentic-voice`, `proof-policy`,
`organic-first`.
