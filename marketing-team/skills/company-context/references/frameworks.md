# Frameworks (Frameworks Social)

## What it sells
The agency Josh started in 2024, which grew into "Nerativ / Frameworks".
Reddit content operations and organic visibility: original seeded posts on
high-priority keywords, peer-level replies in existing threads, persona
accounts warmed before use, owned threads and subreddits, keyword "takeovers",
SEO and AI-citation programs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews,
Gemini). Sold as monthly programs or fixed-price special projects (a launch, a
seasonal push, a partner campaign, a comparison narrative).

Delivery team includes writers and community managers, many working persona
accounts. Every post and reply is reported monthly through our own reporting
platform.

## Who buys
From current accounts: mobile measurement and analytics, small-business and
fintech apps (including accountant/bookkeeper audiences in the US), AI and
workflow tools, B2B SaaS. Marketing and growth leads who want to show up in
peer conversations and AI answers. Some programs are B2C-adjacent.

## The wedge
Retention. Most Reddit "programs" get purged within a week because they post
like vendors. Ours stay up (85-90%+) because accounts are warmed, writers know
the domain, and most posts mention nothing. Plus full transparency: every post
and result shared.

## Proof bank

business), stated as "the posts our team has created and placed":

- 85-90%+ post retention.
