# Nerativ

## What it sells
B2B demand generation and content for buyers who now research in AI answers
and community threads. AEO/GEO (getting cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude,
Gemini, Google AI Overviews), Reddit and community programs, owned communities,
SEO/GEO articles, review programs (G2, Trustpilot), AI visibility audits and
tracking. Monthly retainer, land narrow and expand.

Pricing (internal, never in public copy unless told): standard retainer
$10-12k/mo, ICP benchmark $12-14k/mo, a reduced 3-month trial at $8k/mo for the
right new logo, 6-12 month contracts.

## Who buys
B2B SaaS and tech, $10M+ revenue, roughly 50 to 2,000 people. Cybersecurity
(strongest vertical), fintech, legal tech, cloud/DevOps, insurance. Already
running content marketing, often multi-product.

- Budget owner: VP or Director of Marketing.
- Closest to the pain: Head of Content, Content Marketing Lead.
- Cares about attribution: Head of Demand Gen / Growth.
- Signs: CMO, or CEO at smaller companies.
- Champions: Product Marketing (picks which product leads), Brand/Comms (voice and risk).

Buying trigger: competitors getting cited by LLMs while they aren't; a product
technical enough that generic brand content doesn't land with practitioners;
tired of strategy decks.

## The wedge
We run the accounts and write the content. Real people with real domain
backgrounds (ex-AWS engineers, developers, security people, actuaries, a
biochemistry master's for healthcare). No bots, no AI posting, ever. Buyers who
want a strategy deck and a dashboard should hire someone else, and we say so.
